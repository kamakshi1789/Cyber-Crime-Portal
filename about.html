About Cyber Secure India

National Awareness & Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

🛡 Cyber Security Initiative

Building a Safer Digital India

Cyber Secure India is a national cyber awareness and complaint reporting portal designed to help citizens stay protected against online frauds, phishing attacks, fake calls, UPI scams, identity theft, and social media crimes.

🎯 Our Mission

To spread cyber awareness, reduce digital fraud, and provide secure cyber crime reporting facilities for citizens across India.

🛡 Citizen Protection

We educate users about phishing attacks, fake calls, UPI scams, deepfake frauds, and identity theft techniques.

📢 Awareness Programs

The portal provides awareness campaigns, SOP guidance, cyber alerts, and scam prevention techniques.

Why Cyber Awareness Matters?


Cyber criminals are using advanced technologies like AI voice cloning, phishing links, fake investment platforms, malicious applications, and social engineering techniques to target citizens.


Awareness is the strongest defense against cyber crime. This portal encourages safe online practices and helps users report incidents quickly for faster investigation and response.
