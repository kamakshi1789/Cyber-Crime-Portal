Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Follow these critical steps immediately upon discovering a cyber fraud.

Cyber fraud victims should act immediately. Delays in reporting may reduce the chances of recovering lost money.

1. Immediate Action

Call the National Cyber Helpline 1930 instantly. Contact your bank and request account blocking to stop unauthorized transactions.

🛡 Important

  • Act within first few hours.
  • Freeze suspicious transactions.
  • Keep transaction IDs ready.

2. Preserve Evidence

Save screenshots, call recordings, emails, fake profiles, transaction slips, and suspicious links as evidence.

🛡 Important

  • Do not delete chats.
  • Take screenshots immediately.
  • Store files safely.

3. Secure Your Accounts

Change passwords of compromised accounts and enable two-factor authentication for better security protection.

🛡 Important

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable 2FA security.
  • Avoid password reuse.

4. Report Officially

Submit your complaint using the cyber crime portal along with transaction proof and supporting evidence.

🛡 Important

  • Provide accurate information.
  • Upload evidence properly.
  • Track complaint regularly.

5. Scan Your Device

Run antivirus scans and remove malicious applications, APK files, or suspicious browser extensions.

🛡 Important

  • Use trusted antivirus software.
  • Remove unknown apps.
  • Update device regularly.

6. Stay Aware

Learn about emerging cyber fraud techniques and educate family members about online safety.

🛡 Important

  • Follow cyber news updates.
  • Educate elderly family members.
  • Verify before trusting online offers.
